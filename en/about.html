About me

About

Colombian. More than eight years in Mexico. First Growth hire at Clara. Today General Manager for Mexico and Colombia. Father.

Path

I joined Clara from the beginning. I was the company’s first Growth hire. Later I led the launch and growth of Clara Colombia. Today I am Country Manager for Mexico and Colombia.

My focus has always been the same: building real growth engines — sales, partnerships, product — with P&L responsibility.

Family

I live in Mexico City with my wife and our daughter. We build the household with the same intentionality I use in business: clear systems, explicit priorities, and shared leadership.

I believe leadership is not split between office and home. It is practiced in both.

What I am building

I am positioning myself as one of the references in tech-financial leadership in LatAm — not only as a corporate executive, but as someone who also leads at home.

I publish consistently (at least once a week) on business, fintech, and family systems.
